**Why a data license and not GPL?** Rules are data, not code. The value of a rule
feed is its freshness and its update pipeline — not the static file. A permissive
attribution license lets defenders (and future paid-feed customers) adopt the packs
without license friction, exactly like the Greenbone/OpenVAS and Sigma/DRL precedents.
